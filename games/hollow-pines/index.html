How to Play

  • Move — WASD or arrow keys · left side of the screen on phones
  • Look — mouse (click the game first) · drag the right side on phones
  • Run — Shift · RUN button. Watch your stamina.
  • Use / pick up — E · USE. Some things need you to hold it.
  • Shoot — left click · FIRE. Reload — R. Switch weapon — Q or 1, 2, 3 · SWAP
  • Flashlight — F · LIGHT. Keep the beam right on him and he backs off, but it overheats.
  • Follow the red ◆ on the compass. Weapons and ammo are hidden in old sheds around the forest.
  • Checkpoints save at every objective. If you die, you go back to the last one. You have 3 save slots.
