Add SkyCast to Home Screen
  1. Tap the button above, then tap Allow.
  2. Open Settings and tap Profile Downloaded near the top.
  3. Tap Install, enter your passcode, and tap Install again.
  4. The SkyCast icon is now on your Home Screen.
Settings will say the profile is "Not Verified". That's normal for small apps: it only adds the SkyCast icon, nothing else. You can remove it any time in Settings › General › VPN & Device Management.
Or open SkyCast in Safari
Other way: open SkyCast in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  1. In Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
