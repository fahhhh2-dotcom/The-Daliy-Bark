Firefighters Climb Down a Steep Hill to Rescue Chester the Dog

In Pierce County, Washington, a dog named Chester went on a bit too big of an adventure on Saturday. He slipped down a steep hillside and ended up about 60 feet below the top — that's about as tall as a six-story building!

Chester wasn't hurt, but the hill was so steep that he couldn't climb back up by himself. So Central Pierce Fire & Rescue was called to help. Firefighters carefully made their way down the steep, tricky ground to reach the stranded pup and bring him back up.

At the top of the hill, Chester was reunited with his owner. Photos from the rescue show a happy dog who looked more than ready to be back with his family. The firefighters said they're always glad to help when a pet is in trouble.

💡
Fun fact: A dog's paw pads are thick and a little rough, which helps them grip rocks, dirt and uneven ground. But on a really steep slope, even four paws can slip!

Source: KIRO 7 Seattle, published November 23, 2026

Breed Spotlight

Bernese Mountain Dog

Bernese Mountain Dogs come from the mountains of Switzerland, where they pulled carts and helped on farms. Their big, sturdy bodies and thick tri-color coats make them great at hiking hills and handling cold weather. They're gentle, loving dogs that are famous for being great with kids.
