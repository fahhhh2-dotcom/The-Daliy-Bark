Firefighters Help Rescue Archie the Lost Dog From a Virginia Mountainside

Archie, a Golden Labrador Retriever, went missing for several days in Bent Mountain, Virginia. His owner, Betsy Hill Hudson, works at Angels of Assisi, a group that rescues animals and helps them find homes.

Betsy and other helpers searched the area and called out Archie's name. Then they heard crying coming from far down the mountain! Animal Control asked Roanoke County fire and rescue crews to help reach the tricky spot where Archie was stuck.

Captain John Richardson of Hollins Fire Station #5 said Archie was mostly just worn out, and he had probably gone days without water. A volunteer from Angels of Assisi gave him fluids through an IV to help him feel better, and Archie was happily back with Betsy again.

💡
Fun fact: Dogs can hear sounds about four times farther away than people can, so a lost dog may hear its name being called from far away.

Source: WSLS 10, published November 14, 2026
