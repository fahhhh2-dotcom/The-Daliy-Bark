Thousands of Dogs Head to Mexico City for a Big Four-Day Dog Show

Dogs from across North America are strutting their stuff in Mexico City this week. The American Kennel Club (AKC) and the Federación Canófila Mexicana (FCM), Mexico's official kennel club, teamed up for four days of dog shows, running November 12 through 15.

The shows drew 2,856 entries, which means thousands of chances for a dog to win a ribbon. There are all-breed shows, where every kind of dog can compete, and specialty shows for single breeds.

The leader of the AKC called it a historic milestone for the dog-show world, since it's a big step for the two kennel clubs working together across the border.

💡
Fun fact: At a dog show, judges don't compare dogs to each other first. They compare each dog to the "perfect" version of its breed, then pick the dog that comes closest.
